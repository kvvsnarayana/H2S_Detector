FUNCTIONAL SAFETY & SCOPE

Safety Considerations & Design Scope

Clear guidelines regarding the physical sensing badge and system capabilities.

🛡️

Gas-Permeable Membrane Protection

The thin protective membrane allows H₂S gas access to the chemical sensing strip while helping protect the sensing substrate from liquid water splash, sweat, and environmental dust. It is designed for ambient industrial workplace screening.

⚠️

Colorimetric Screening Scope

Sulfide Sentinels provides passive chemical exposure assessment and digital safety management logging. It is designed to complement—not replace—certified personal electronic real-time gas alarms and facility safety procedures.
